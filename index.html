    Welcome to All Students

    First of all, congratulations to those who have passed the High School Diploma examination during the reform era of the Ministry of
    Education, Youth and Sport. However, we also encourage those who did not pass not to lose hope in their studies, as you can still
    enroll in higher education institutions in all majors just like those who passed, by studying for an Associate Degree and then transferring
    credits to pursue a Bachelor's Degree. We would like to inform all of you that Western University is on the right path in the education
    sector by prioritizing teaching and learning quality, curriculum, discipline, and services as core strategies for training. Western University's
    curriculum also integrates both Hard Skills and Soft Skills for every student. As a result, in recent years, the number of students enrolling
    at Western University has continued to increase steadily, even though the number of students passing the Baccalaureate exam has
    decreased following the reforms by the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sport.
